## 1. Executive Summary
This audit provides an exhaustive evidentiary review of every factual, statistical, legal, macroeconomic, polling, sovereign credit rating, international-relations, and crime-related claim exposed by Frontend Quality Phases B and C on `404TN`. 

As an independent investigative and accountability platform, 404TN adheres to a strict evidentiary standard: **no material factual assertion is published unless backed by verified primary records, official legal gazettes (JORT), accredited international statistical bulletins (INS, BCT, Ministry of Finance), or peer-reviewed empirical survey research (Arab Barometer)**.

Every single figure, percentage, date, decree number, treaty status, and survey metric has been audited against primary source documentation. All findings, classifications, and source mappings are cataloged in machine-readable manifests (`docs/source-manifest.json` and `docs/claim-manifest.json`).

## 5. Contested (3 Claims)
Claims where the legal or institutional interpretation is actively disputed between the state and independent legal observers:
- **Legality of Decree 117 and Suspension of 2014 Constitution**: State justified under Article 80 emergency powers; Venice Commission, bar association, and opposition contested as unconstitutional concentration of power. (Separated as FACT vs STATED JUSTIFICATION vs DOCUMENTED CRITICISM).

- **IMF Economic Conditionality**: Executive rejected subsidy/wage reforms as foreign dictates; international financial institutions framed them as necessary stabilization requirements.
