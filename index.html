GABÈS / ENVIRONMENT · FLAGSHIP INVESTIGATION

The city paying the price of pollution.

For decades, Gabès has carried the environmental cost of Tunisia’s industrial model. In 2026, pollution, public health, industrial policy and political accountability remain inseparable.

■ Industrial pollution
■ Marine ecosystem
■ Public health
■ Local testimony
■ Government promises
■ State response

The Groupe Chimique Tunisien (GCT) industrial complex at Chatt Essalam produces phosphoric acid and fertilizer essential to Tunisia's export balance. The resulting by-product, phosphogypsum, has created a dead zone across the inner Gulf of Gabès.

In June 2017, the Cabinet approved a formal dismantling and relocation decision. In Summer 2026, all coastal units remain operational. Continuous real-time atmospheric sensor data is not publicly published by state agencies.

DOCUMENTARY CHRONOLOGY · 2019-2026

Power, promises and responsibility.

Since 2021, political authority in Tunisia has become increasingly concentrated around the presidency. 404TN examines what this concentration of power has produced in practice — and how President Kais Saied and state institutions have responded to the country’s major crises.

THE RESULTS

Direct Responsibility Tested by Compounding Crises

2026
DOCUMENTED CONTEXT

Five years following the July 2021 rupture, all institutional mechanisms are directly accountable to the presidency. 404TN documents how this centralized governance model performs when confronted with the compounding infrastructural stress of water shortages, energy load shedding, inflation, and environmental degradation in Gabès.

EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVES
  • ▪Direct presidential administration of utility crises (SONEDE, STEG, GCT, Transport)
  • ▪Attribution of supply shortages to market hoarders and bureaucratic sabotage
  • ▪Bilateral security-focused Mediterranean border agreements with European counterparts
INSTITUTIONAL OUTCOME: Every administrative delay, utility disruption, and economic indicator maps directly to presidential responsibility.

METHODOLOGY & PROVENANCE

Evidence, not slogans.

Every major claim published by 404TN should be traceable to a source, document, dataset, public statement, photograph, video or testimony.

01 · OFFICIAL DATA

Official Data

JORT decrees, INS statistical bulletins, ONAGRI water balances, STEG electrical dispatch registries, and BCT reserve reports.

02 · DOCUMENTS

Documents

Court filings, ministerial circulars, procurement audits, NGO research papers (FTDES, SNJT, ONAT), and international treaty texts.

03 · LOCAL TESTIMONY

Local Testimony

Corroborated interviews with residents, farmers, fishermen in Gabès, civil servants, legal counsel, and labor union delegates.

04 · INDEPENDENT REPORTING

Independent Reporting

Field documentation, satellite imagery analysis, maritime tracking transponders (AIS), and on-site photo/video verification.

THREE-TIER EPISTEMIC CLASSIFICATION
FACT

Directly supported by official records, empirical datasets, legal decrees, or multi-source verified documentation.

CLAIM

A public statement made by an official, ministry, institution, or witness that has not yet been independently verified.

ANALYSIS

404TN editorial or causal interpretation synthesizing documented facts, historical baselines, and institutional records.
